# Supply and operations

Recommendations here are our judgement unless a source is named in [06-evidence.md](06-evidence.md). Legal points are marked **[CA]**.

## 1. Why a good guide would choose us

Supplier complaints about the large platforms are consistent: commission raised without consent, slow payouts, opaque ranking, and paying to rank.

**The pitch:** "Your price in rupees, guaranteed. 20% fixed in writing for 24 months. We build your listing with you on WhatsApp. Tips are 100% yours."

**How we explain the commission, in plain words**

> You tell us what you want to earn, say ₹8,500. We add our share on top and the traveller pays about $125. From our share we pay the card fees, advertising and customer support. You pay nothing to join and nothing if there is no booking. Your ₹8,500 is fixed in rupees whatever the dollar does. We pay your bank or UPI within 48 hours of the tour, once the card payment has settled. Tips are yours.

Suppliers think in a net rupee amount, so that is how the product speaks to them. The traveller never sees the split.

## 3. Verification

**Before a listing is published**

- Phone verified; selfie; signed terms.
- For any tour inside a protected monument: licence checked, and the "monument-eligible" flag set.

**Before the first payout**

- PAN.
- Aadhaar through DigiLocker; the raw number is never stored.
- Bank account or UPI confirmed by a small test transfer with a name match.
- GST number, or a turnover declaration.

**By supplier type**

| Type | Documents |
|---|---|
| Guide | Regional Level Guide card, IITFC certificate, or state licence (UP, Rajasthan) |
| Agency | GST number; tourism-ministry recognition or IATO membership shown as a badge, not required |
| Vehicle | Registration, commercial permit, insurance, fitness certificate, driver's licence **[CA: permit rules not verified]** |

- Police verification: self-declared at launch; a certificate earns a "Verified+" badge.
- Verification services cost roughly ₹15–40 per supplier (an estimate; no provider publishes a full rate card).

## 4. Standards every listing accepts

1. No shopping or commission stop unless it is written in the itinerary. A breach refunds the traveller.
2. No pressure for tips.
3. A licensed guide inside monuments.
4. A commercial vehicle, with the actual car and driver named the evening before.
5. The tour delivered matches the listing.
6. The supplier's public direct price is not below our price. We do not police other platforms at launch.

**For women travellers:** verified ID shown before the tour; live trip sharing; a female-guide filter; no itinerary change after dark without consent in the app; an emergency WhatsApp number.

## 5. Running it as one person

- **Vetting:** a 15-minute video call (English, licence shown on camera). The first 20 Agra suppliers are shadowed or mystery-shopped in person.
- **Incidents:** one form, three severities. For a safety incident: suspend at once, refund first, investigate after.
- **Reviews:** verified bookings only; suppliers can reply publicly; negative reviews are published.
- **Tiers:** New → Verified → Top Local (20+ tours, 4.8+, under 2% cancellations), which earns faster payout and instant booking.
- **Strikes:** a no-show or an undisclosed shop stop is a strike; two means removal.
- **Supplier cancellations:** no fines at launch; lower ranking, and the rebooking cost recovered from the next payout.

**The gap to plan for:** round-the-clock support with one founder. At launch, state support hours honestly, make the no-show refund automatic, and add cover before peak season.

## 6. Keeping bookings on the platform

At 20%, some leakage is accepted. What reduces it:

- Contact details are masked in chat until the booking is confirmed.
- Reviews and ranking only accumulate here.
- Payment protection and reliable payout.
- A lower rate, 10–12%, on repeat or "bring your own customer" bookings through the supplier's own link.

Action is taken only on flagrant solicitation: a warning, then removal.

## 7. The first 50 suppliers

**Where:** approved-guide associations in Agra, Delhi and Jaipur; the tourism ministry's regional lists; independents rated 4.9+ on Tripadvisor; guides active on Instagram; concierges at boutique hotels and homestays; IITFC alumni groups.

**Agreement essentials [CA]:** independent contractor and marketplace status; supplier responsible for licences and taxes; cancellation and no-show terms; indemnity; insurance; a non-exclusive licence to use their photos with a warranty that they own them; data protection; electronic signature.
